## Fonts

`frontend/public/fonts/` holds the latin subsets of two Google Fonts, both under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org):

- **Inter** — Rasmus Andersson
- **Instrument Serif** — Rodrigo Fuenzalida

They are vendored rather than loaded from a CDN so the app has no third-party
runtime dependency and renders identically offline.
